Why Explainability (XAI) in Financial Forecasting?

Black-box machine learning algorithms like Deep LSTMs provide high non-linear capacity but lack transparency. Stakeholders and risk managers cannot verify why a specific price trend or directional shift is predicted.

SHAP (SHapley Additive exPlanations) provides mathematically grounded local and global feature attribution:

Temporal Lag Attribution

Quantify which past lookback days ($t-1, t-2, \dots, t-k$) contributed most positively or negatively toward the upcoming predicted price.

Auditable Insights

Eliminates reliance on opaque outputs, verifying whether predictions are driven by authentic recent market trends rather than noise.

Planned XAI Architecture
  • Explainer: shap.GradientExplainer or shap.DeepExplainer for Keras LSTM.
  • Background Reference: Sampled slice from historical training sequences.
  • Visualization: Summary force plots and lag importance bar charts rendered dynamically.
