Scientific Evaluation & Discussion
1. Methodology Integrity & Fair Benchmark

Both ARIMA and LSTM models were evaluated on the identical chronological test window of {{ result.test_records }} days. Preprocessing scalers and differencing parameters were strictly isolated to the training partition, eliminating lookahead bias and data leakage.

2. Linear vs. Non-Linear Dynamics

ARIMA is grounded in linear stochastic processes, assuming stationarity after differencing. In contrast, LSTM maintains gated memory cells (input, forget, output gates) capable of approximating non-linear price patterns over sliding historical windows.

Important Academic Conclusion:

A model achieving lower error on a specific holdout window does not guarantee consistent superiority across different market regimes. Cryptocurrency markets exhibit structural breaks, sudden liquidity shifts, and regulatory shocks that can impact statistical models differently over time.
